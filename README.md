A GTA 6 launch-week meme for the make.fun bounty: a Vice City couple
celebrates finally holding GTA 6 — after waiting 13 years, calling in sick
on day one — while make.fun's ghost mascot strolls down the street with
them, star coin in hand. The meme was posted on X with the caption
"WAITED 13 YEARS FOR GTA 6 / CALLED IN SICK ON DAY ONE".

The post's text carries the make.fun bounty link and the entrant's play
code, and tells readers that people and their AI agents earn real money
winning bounties on make.fun.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `POST.json` (the X post's address) to the output directory and
prints nothing on success.
